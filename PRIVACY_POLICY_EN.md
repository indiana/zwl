# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 31, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Legalny Bushcraft** (ZWL) mobile application ("the App") handles information. The App is provided by its developer ("we", "us").

We believe in privacy by design. The App is an offline-first tool that helps you check whether you are in a "Zanocuj w Lesie" (forest camping) zone in Poland.

## Information We Handle

### Location data

- The App uses the device's **precise location** (GPS) so it can show, on a map, whether you are currently inside or outside a "Zanocuj w Lesie" zone.
- Location is read **only while you are using the map** and only with your permission, which you grant through the iOS location prompt.
- Your location is processed **entirely on your device**. We do **not** collect, transmit, upload, or store your location.
- We do **not** associate, link, or combine your location with any account or identifier.

### Offline maps

- The App can download map tiles and forest-zone data to your device so the map works **without an internet connection**.
- Downloaded data is stored locally in the App's sandbox and can be removed at any time (the App includes a "Clear offline cache" option).

## What We Do NOT Collect

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email address, phone number, or any other identifier)
- Account information (the App has **no accounts** and **no sign-in**)
- Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or tracking data
- Usage analytics, crash logs, or diagnostics
- Anything you type, search, or view inside the App

## Third-Party Services

The App does **not** integrate any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Map rendering and offline tile storage run entirely on your device.

The only network activity performed by the App is downloading publicly available map/forest-zone data from public sources (for example, open geodata used to build the offline map), which you initiate or which is required to show the map. We do not control which data you view.

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or share any information with third parties. Because the App does not collect your data in the first place, there is nothing to share.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Since no data leaves your device, there is nothing stored on our servers to retain or delete. Local data (downloaded tiles and cached data) can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's cache or uninstalling the App.

## Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age threshold in your region). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at the URL where you found this document. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
